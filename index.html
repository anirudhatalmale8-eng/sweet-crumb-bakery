Our bakery

Two ovens, one small room, and no shortcuts.

Sweet Crumb started as a home oven and a stubborn sourdough starter we still feed twice a day. Everything on the shelf is made here — no factory mixes, no preservatives, nothing baked yesterday and warmed up today.

The bread is fermented for twenty-four hours, which is why it keeps for days and sits light. The croissants are laminated by hand with Amul butter, folded three times and proofed overnight. And the mawa cake is the one our grandmother made for Diwali, unchanged.
